Executive Summary

Tercet Organics is not just another wellness brand. We are the custodians of ceremonial-grade matcha in Nigeria — a product category that is both a superfood and a status symbol.

Our mission is clear: To position Tercet Organics Matcha as the definitive premium wellness product for the elite demographic of Lagos and Abuja. To cultivate a luxury community around the ritual of matcha: one that transcends tea and becomes a lifestyle marker of calm, performance, and prestige.

This 12-month marketing strategy outlines how we will combine content, community, and commerce to drive sustainable growth, build loyalty, and ensure Tercet Organics becomes synonymous with luxury matcha in West Africa.

Brand Positioning

Core Promise

"Discover the Power of Matcha — Purity, Prestige, Performance."

Target Audience

Affluent men and women aged 25–55 in Lagos and Abuja.

Psychographic Profile

Health-conscious, image-conscious, status-driven. Value exclusivity and global wellness trends.

Differentiator

Strategic Pillars

Our five-pillar approach to market dominance

1

Content Leadership

We will educate, inspire, and convert through carefully curated organic and paid content.

  • Instagram & TikTok: 3–5 posts per week, 70% video content
  • Content Types: Ritual & beauty positioning, recipes & hacks
  • Blog & SEO: Bi-monthly articles for local search dominance
  • Lead Magnet: "The Elite Guide to Matcha" eBook
2

Paid Growth with Discipline

Advertising will be lean, premium, and ROI-driven.

  • Ad Budget: ₦200,000/month from Month 2
  • Instagram/Facebook: 60% - visual storytelling
  • TikTok: 30% - virality & community
  • Google Search: 10% - high-intent buyers
  • CPA Target: ₦5,000–₦7,000
  • ROAS: ≥ 7:1
3

Elite Community Building

We will create not just buyers, but belonging through Tercet Circle.

  • Launch: Tercet Circle (Month 3)
  • Exclusive WhatsApp group for elite members
  • Quarterly wellness events & brunches
  • Access to nutritionists & lifestyle coaches
  • Goal: 600+ active members by Year-End
  • This community is our brand moat.
4

Events & Influencer Strategy

Leverage authority and aspiration to reinforce premium positioning.

  • Monthly IG Lives/Webinars (₦50k/session)
  • Quarterly Premium Sessions (₦100k/quarter)
  • Micro-influencer partnerships (2k–10k followers)
  • Sampling at luxury gyms & spas in Ikoyi, Lekki
  • Partnerships with wellness retreats
5

Customer Retention & Loyalty

Retention is the backbone of profitability.

  • Subscription Plans: 10% discount for monthly refills
  • Referral Program: ₦2,000 credit per referral
  • Bundles: Matcha + whisk + recipe guide
  • VIP Access: Early access to new blends
  • Goal: 50% repeat buyers by Month 12
